================================================================================
  WHY THIS APPROACH IS BETTER
================================================================================

OLD PROBLEM: "SELL signals" from low buy-confidence
❌ Model only trained on "is this a buy?"
❌ Using inverted confidence as "sell" is dishonest
❌ Confuses retail investors
❌ Not production-ready

NEW SOLUTION: Three honest layers
✅ Layer 1: Model predicts ENTRY (buy/sell/hold)
✅ Layer 2: Rules predict EXIT (time/stop-loss/decay)
✅ Layer 3: Model predicts TRUE SELLS (Phase 2)
✅ All transparent to users
✅ Production-ready architecture

RESULT: Retail investors get:
1. Clear ENTRY guidance ("should I buy?")
2. Clear EXIT guidance ("when should I exit?")
3. Clear SELL guidance ("is this going down?")
4. ZERO jargon - plain English explanations
5. Full transparency on model accuracy
